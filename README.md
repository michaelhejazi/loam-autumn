# Autumn

A theme for [Loam](https://github.com/loamhq/loam), drawn from golden leaves in daylight and a red maple at dusk.

Autumn dresses all of Loam in its own colours, in light and in dark, with
Young Serif for its headings. Its cover changes with the light: a daytime picture in light, a dusk one in dark.

## Choices

In Settings › Appearance, once Autumn is on:

- **Agent faces**: Signature, Each its own, Circle (the default), Rounded square
- **Inside**: Mark (the default), Initials

## What it touches

Nothing but how Loam looks. It talks to no one on the network, reads no
thread or memory, and runs no program on your server.

## Getting it

In your Loam, open Settings › Market, find Autumn, press **Get**, then
**Use**. Or install it from Settings › Plugins with its link:

```
https://github.com/michaelhejazi/loam-autumn/tree/v1.0.2
```
